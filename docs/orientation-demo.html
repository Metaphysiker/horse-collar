Tilt = √(pitch²+roll²)
Pitch — the chip tilts forward or backward (nose up/down on horse).
Roll — the chip tilts left or right (ear to shoulder on horse).
Tilt — total deviation from flat. Combines both using Pythagoras. Always positive. Doesn't care which direction.

When flat on table: all three are 0°.
When standing on its side: one of pitch/roll is 90°, tilt is 90°.
When tilted diagonally: tilt is larger than either pitch or roll alone.
